DizTech ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how you can ask us about or remove it.

## Information we collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the details you provide: your name, email address, phone number, company, job title, the service you're interested in, and any message you write. We don't collect this information anywhere else on the site, and we don't require an account or login to browse it.

## How we use it

We use your contact details to respond to your enquiry, discuss your project, and follow up on the services you asked about. We don't sell, rent, or trade your information to third parties, and we don't use it for advertising.

## Where it's stored

Form submissions are handled through our website hosting provider's form service and stored securely on their infrastructure. Standard measures, including spam filtering, help keep submissions genuine.

## Cookies

This site doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. Some functionality, such as our content management system, may set essential cookies needed for that feature to work.

## Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time. Contact us using the details on our Contact page and we'll respond promptly.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in how the site works. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

## Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your information, reach out through the contact form on this site or via the details on our Contact page.
